Works Cited

Master bibliography and source register for the R Theory website and research program.

Purpose. This page records external sources used to support, contextualize, test, or challenge claims published in R Theory. It is the shared destination for literature matches identified during research reviews, including searches conducted with Consensus.

Editorial standard. A source is not evidence for a claim merely because it is related to the topic. Each entry identifies the work precisely, provides a stable link or identifier, states its R Theory use, and distinguishes direct support from background context or disagreement.

Current research boundary. The sources below support the established 21 cm hyperfine/Zeeman measurement context, provide related work on chirality and physical symmetry selection, and document general orthogonal-group tensor-projection methods relevant to Book 17 §17.18. None establishes the C-1 sector-sign bridge or the specific SO(16)→SO(10)×SO(6) 1820→135→54 construction. The physical C-1 bridge and manuscript-specific Q4/P54 normalization remain open.

Verified references

Furlanetto, Steven R. “Theoretical Framework: The Fundamentals of the 21 cm Line.” In The Cosmic 21-cm Revolution: Charting the First Billion Years of Our Universe, edited by Andrei Mesinger, AAS–IOP Astronomy, IOP Publishing, 2019, chapter 1. https://doi.org/10.1088/2514-3433/ab4a73ch1. Preprint: arXiv:1909.13740.

Verified full chapter R Theory use: Book 23 §2, neutral-hydrogen hyperfine transition parameters. Evidence locator: chapter 1 §1.1 and the radiative-transfer / Einstein-coefficient relations. The chapter gives ν21 ≈ 1420.4057 MHz, A10 ≈ 2.85 × 10−15 s−1, and g1/g0 = 3. It supports the standard physical context, not the sector-occupancy claim.

Cooray, Asantha, and Steven R. Furlanetto. “Polarization Signals of the 21-cm Background from the Era of Reionization.” Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society: Letters, vol. 359, no. 1, 2005, pp. L47–L52. DOI: 10.1111/j.1745-3933.2005.00035.x. Preprint: astro-ph/0408314.

Verified full article R Theory use: Book 23 §2, Zeeman σ components and circular polarization. Evidence locator: Introduction and “Method of Calculation,” equations (1)–(3). The article explains opposite circular polarizations for the Zeeman σ components viewed along the magnetic field and the use of their differential intensity to probe the line-of-sight field. It does not identify that observable with R Theory's sector sign.

Wolfe, Arthur M., Regina A. Jorgenson, Timothy Robishaw, Carl Heiles, and J. Xavier Prochaska. “Spectral Polarization of the Redshifted 21 cm Absorption Line Toward 3C 286.” The Astrophysical Journal, vol. 733, no. 1, article 24, 2011. DOI: 10.1088/0004-637X/733/1/24. Preprint: arXiv:1103.4167.

Verified full paper R Theory use: Book 23 §2, polarimetric calibration and falsification discipline. Evidence locator: §§2.1–2.3 and §4. The authors withdrew an earlier Zeeman detection after finding a phase/software error that interchanged Stokes V and U; the corrected data gave an upper limit rather than the claimed detection. This is a methodological caution, not evidence for either R Theory sector.

Quack, Martin, Georg Seyfang, and Gunther Wichmann. “Perspectives on Parity Violation in Chiral Molecules: Theory, Spectroscopic Experiment and Biomolecular Homochirality.” Chemical Science, vol. 13, no. 36, 2022, pp. 10598–10643. DOI: 10.1039/D2SC01323A.

Verified full review R Theory use: Book 0 §15 and Corollary 0.III.C2; background on physical parity selection. Evidence locator: abstract and §§2–4. The review reports theoretically predicted parity-violating energy differences between molecular enantiomers but states that they had not yet been detected experimentally at publication. This is related context, not a proof of R Theory's global sector assignment.

Kishine, Jun-ichiro, Hiroaki Kusunose, and Hiroshi M. Yamamoto. “On the Definition of Chirality and Enantioselective Fields.” Israel Journal of Chemistry, vol. 62, nos. 11–12, article e202200049, 2022. DOI: 10.1002/ijch.202200049.

Verified full article R Theory use: Book 0 §15 / Book 23 §3, related order-parameter and physical-field formulations. Evidence locator: §§2 and 5. The authors formulate an electric toroidal monopole G0 as an order parameter for molecular/crystal chirality and discuss enantioselective fields. This is a domain-specific related formulation, not a universal selector for R Theory's doubled carrier.

Helpin, Thomas. Décompositions irréductibles des tenseurs via l’algèbre de Brauer et applications à la gravitation métrique-affine [“Irreducible Decompositions of Tensors via the Brauer Algebra and Applications to Metric-Affine Gravity”]. PhD thesis, Université de Tours, defended 19 December 2023; arXiv:2407.18019v1, 25 July 2024. DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2407.18019. arXiv:2407.18019.

Verified full doctoral thesis R Theory use: Book 17 §17.18, orthogonal-group tensor decomposition and trace-free projector methodology. Evidence locator: chapter 3 §3.2.4 (universal traceless projector), §§3.3.1–3.3.3 ((f+1)-traceless projectors), and chapter 4 §4.5. The thesis develops Brauer-algebra projectors for O(d) tensor spaces and arbitrary trace-free decompositions. It is mathematical background, not a derivation of the specific SO(16) spinor branching or the manuscript’s Q4/P54 normalization.

Citation and review policy

  • Preserve the source's actual claim; do not strengthen it in paraphrase.
  • Prefer original papers, authoritative datasets, standards, and primary documentation over tertiary summaries.
  • Record disagreements and negative results as carefully as supporting results.
  • Do not cite a Consensus-generated summary as a substitute for the underlying publication.
  • Keep literature assessment separate from R Theory's own proofs, assertions, imports, and open questions.
  • When a source is relevant to a specific page or book, link the citation back to that location.
